## About

The Adobe Workfront Fusion MCP Server brings Fusion into your favorite AI assistant, such as Adobe Coworker, Claude and more. Instead of switching between tools, just ask your assistant to help with your Fusion work in plain language. Getting started is simple: sign in with your Adobe ID and choose the Fusion organization you want to work in. From then on, your assistant can find and review your scenarios, help you understand how they work, organize your folders and take care of everyday tasks for you. Your data stays secure. The assistant only sees and does what you're already allowed to do in Fusion, and your Adobe credentials are never shared with the AI tool. We regularly add new capabilities, so your assistant can do more in Fusion over time with no extra setup on your side.
